About TherapyBytes

TherapyBytes is a free documentation aid. Pick a client's DSM-5-TR diagnosis and the app surfaces symptom prompts, evidence-based interventions, outcome measures, goal templates, and documentation tips, then assembles a structured note and checks it against common best practices.

Important limits

  • It does not diagnose. Symptom lists are brief paraphrased prompts, not the DSM-5-TR criteria. Diagnose from the DSM-5-TR itself.
  • Verify ICD-10-CM codes and CPT/time rules against current manuals and payer requirements.
  • Suggestions are starting points. Your clinical judgment, supervision, ethics code, and state law govern what goes in a record.
  • Not legal advice. Progress notes are part of the legal medical record; HIPAA "psychotherapy notes" are a separate, optional category kept apart from the record.

Your data

Drafts are saved only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Clear all on shared computers. Avoid entering names or other identifiers.
